# A practical first institutional conversation (research only)

**Goal:** obtain an academic/clinical research collaboration, not immediate deployment or certification.

1. Ask the institution's biomedical engineering department, research office, or clinical PI whether they would consider a **retrospective algorithm-development study**, initially using publicly available deidentified VTaC examples rather than hospital records.
2. Share `README.md`, `regulatory/AUDIT_GAP_REPORT.md`, `regulatory/CLINICAL_EVALUATION_PLAN_TEMPLATE.md`, current test report, intended-use statement and code provenance. Explicitly disclose that the AI model is not trained on adequate data and no regulated authorizations exist.
3. Ask the sponsor/site whether this study needs **Institutional Ethics Committee review** or a formal written exemption/determination. Do not decide that independently.
4. If a future study needs hospital data, obtain the institution's data access agreement, consent/waiver decision, secure storage environment, principal investigator, approved dataset selection and QA oversight **before accessing data**.
5. Engage a device regulatory-affairs specialist to document the exact CDSCO pathway for the proposed intended use. A clinician seeing the demo does not authorize clinical investigation.
6. Begin by demonstrating **waveform provenance, fault injection, no AI output without model evidence, test reports and safety limitations**. No bedside devices or patient management.

**Where to apply:** regulatory classification and permissions: https://cdscomdonline.gov.in/ ; applicable test licence: https://www.nsws.gov.in/ ; EC registration details: https://ethics.ncdirindia.org/Registration_of_EC_reviewing.aspx ; CTRI: https://ctri.nic.in/Clinicaltrials/faq.php ; QMS certification body accreditation: https://nabcb.qci.org.in/management-system-certification/ .
